# Cena 3 - Corredor para o close do S110

## Objetivo
Criar um unico take continuo partindo da composicao lateral do corredor e terminando em um close frontal do S110. O enquadramento final deve mostrar somente o display e a parede lisa da casa, sem sofa, mesa, caixa, corredor, porta ou estrutura preta.

## Referencias a anexar
1. `public/media/Sonare-Corredor.png` como quadro inicial e autoridade da arquitetura, parede lisa, iluminacao, corredor, caixa de piso, S110 e porcao visivel do telao.
2. `public/media/sonare-display-s110.png` como autoridade do produto, gabinete, proporcao, interface, cores e textura da parede.

Nao anexar `Sonare-Fachada.png`, `Sonare-Living.png` nem videos anteriores nesta geracao.

## Configuracao obrigatoria
- Modelo: Seedance 2.0.
- Preset: General.
- Duracao: 8s.
- Formato: 16:9.
- Resolucao: 4K.
- Bitrate: High.
- Audio: desligado.
- `Use free gens`: ligado.
- Custo mostrado no botao Generate: `0`. Nao gerar se aparecer outro valor.

## Ritmo do take
| Tempo | Acao |
| --- | --- |
| 0-1.5s | Comecar exatamente em `Sonare-Corredor.png`, com a camera calma. |
| 1.5-4s | Fazer tracking/push-in muito lento em direcao ao S110 instalado na parede lisa. |
| 4-6.5s | Aproximar e alinhar gradualmente a camera de frente para o display. |
| 6.5-8s | Assentar em close estavel mostrando apenas o S110 e a parede lisa. |

## Prompt unico
Colar o prompt e a lista `Avoid` juntos no mesmo campo.

```text
Create one original, seamless, single continuous 8-second architectural product shot using only the two attached Sonare project stills.

Reference image 1 is the exact required opening frame and the authority for the lateral corridor, smooth warm off-white walls, black structural frames, floorstanding speaker, projected city image and S110 position. Reference image 2 is the authority for the exact S110 housing, screen proportions, interface layout, colors and smooth wall texture.

Begin exactly in reference image 1. Perform a very slow, smooth stabilized tracking move and controlled push-in toward the single wall-mounted S110. Gradually align the camera almost perpendicular to the display. Finish in a tighter frontal close-up than the contextual reference: the final frame must contain only the S110 and uninterrupted smooth warm off-white wall. No corridor, black structural frame, door, floor, ceiling, sofa, table, speaker or other equipment may remain visible in the final composition.

Keep the S110 fixed to the same wall throughout. Preserve its exact black housing, proportions and supplied interface. Keep the time, weather area, house image and bottom control categories stable and readable. The interface must not animate, flicker, morph or invent another screen. Photorealistic premium architectural product cinematography, warm controlled indirect lighting, slow deliberate pacing, stabilized 50mm push-in, native 4K detail, no audio.

Avoid: duplicated display, moving display, floating display, changed display position, invented interface, interface morphing, changing icons, random text, unreadable screen, flicker, screen replacement, remote control, charging base, people, hands, silhouettes, cuts, jump cuts, crossfades, montage, teleportation, changed corridor, changed wall, stone wall, warped architecture, sudden acceleration, fast movement, handheld shake, digital zoom, excessive motion blur, neon, daylight, logos, subtitles and watermarks.
```

## Criterios de aprovacao
- Comeca exatamente em `Sonare-Corredor.png`.
- Existe apenas um S110, fixo na mesma parede lisa.
- A interface permanece estavel durante todo o movimento.
- A aproximacao e lenta, frontal e fisicamente plausivel.
- O quadro final mostra somente S110 e parede lisa.
- Nao ha controle remoto, pessoas, cortes, morphing ou objetos inventados.
